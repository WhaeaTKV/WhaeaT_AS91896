1st Encounter:
You finish your drink at the Tavern and wander out the front door. As you do, you stumble and hit the bouncer at the door.
He turns to you angrily and threatens to knock you out.

What do you do?
RUN or FIGHT
Your answer: 







DEFINE list of possible outcomes: ["Strike", "Ball", "Single", "Double", "Triple", "Home Run", "Out"]
SET player score to 0
SET number of outs to 0


WHILE outs are less than 3:
    DISPLAY "Press Enter to swing!"
    WAIT for user input

    RANDOMLY choose outcome from the list
    DISPLAY the outcome

    IF outcome is "Strike" OR "Ball":
        DISPLAY "No hit!"
        CONTINUE to next pitch

    IF outcome is "Out":
        INCREASE outs by 1
        DISPLAY number of outs

    IF outcome is "Single":
        ADD 1 point to score
    IF outcome is "Double":
        ADD 2 points to score
    IF outcome is "Triple":
        ADD 3 points to score
    IF outcome is "Home Run":
        ADD 4 points to score

    DISPLAY current score
